RAVELIN / Aetherline — how to open

1. Double-click Aetherline.uproject
2. Wait for compile if asked (Yes)
3. Press the green Play button

DO NOT place ALArenaBuilder or PlayerStart.
AALGameMode::BeginPlay calls SpawnArenaIfMissing() and builds the yard at Play.
The editor viewport is Unreal's empty default map. That is normal.

Ignore any old 4-step graphic that says "drag a box into the level."
