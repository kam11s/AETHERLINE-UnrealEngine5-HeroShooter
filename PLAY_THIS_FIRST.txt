AETHERLINE — UE 5.8

Download:
https://github.com/kam11s/AETHERLINE-UnrealEngine5-HeroShooter/archive/refs/heads/main.zip

1. Extract to D:\Aetherline  (not inside the Unreal Engine folder)
2. Confirm Source\Aetherline\ALTypes.h contains EALDropPhase
3. Double-click Aetherline.uproject
4. Choose UnrealEditor / UE 5.8
5. Click Yes on rebuild
6. Wait. First compile takes a few minutes.
7. Green Play

Need on this PC: Unreal Engine 5.8 + Visual Studio 2022 (Game development with C++).
If a previous try failed, delete Intermediate and Binaries before opening again.
